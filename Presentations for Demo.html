The Vendor Onboarding Bottleneck

Slide 02: Business Problem & Opportunity

2.1 Problem Statement

  • Manual Document Fatigue: Reviewing 5-10 unstructured vendor PDFs (Tax, Bank, Insurance, Compliance) takes 10-14 days per submission.
  • High Operational Risk: Human oversight misses expired certificates, entity name mismatches, or policy coverage gaps.
  • Inconsistent Resubmissions: Manual follow-up emails for missing/unreadable files lead to endless back-and-forth delays.

2.2 The Opportunity

  • Automated Document Extraction: Instant OCR & parsing of registration forms, tax IDs, and bank proofs.
  • Vector-Grounded Policy Validation: Semantic RAG search matches submission data directly against compliance guidelines.
  • Streamlined Approval Pipeline: Straight-through procurement approval for valid cases; automated escrow for exceptions.

Proposed Solution: AI Onboarding Assistant

Slide 03: Core Platform Capabilities

1. Document Intelligence

Extracts structured fields from registration forms, tax documents, bank proofs, and insurance certificates with multi-document cross-validation.

2. Vector Policy RAG

Embeds and stores compliance rules & checklists in a vector database to perform instant semantic policy grounding and completeness checks.

3. Guided Workflow & Audit

Auto-drafts correction requests for invalid files, routes risks to compliance reviewers, and maintains a complete decision audit trail.

4.1 Happy Path

Straight-Through Approval

All 5-10 docs uploaded, valid expiry dates, matching legal names, and full checklist compliance.

4.2 Resubmission Loop

Correction Request Trigger

Detected expired insurance certificate or mismatch between tax ID and bank registration name.

  • • Identifies exact missing/invalid field
  • • AI auto-drafts specific correction email
  • • Re-evaluates case upon resubmission
Status: CORRECTION REQUESTED
4.3 Policy Exception

Compliance Review Escrow

Insurance policy coverage below standard threshold or non-standard compliance declaration.

  • • RAG flags specific policy deviation
  • • Prepares Risk & Compliance Summary
  • • Routes to Compliance Officer for manual sign-off
Status: ESCALATED TO COMPLIANCE
